Beast AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Beast AI is an AI assistant that lets users interact with AI features through a web interface.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Beast AI may process account information, such as your Google account information when you sign in, and the messages, prompts, or files you choose to submit.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, respond to requests, operate the features you choose, and maintain the service.

Third-party services

Beast AI may use Google Firebase for authentication and other configured services to process requests. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data storage and security

Information may be processed or stored by the services configured for the features you use. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can choose what information you submit and stop using the service at any time. For questions about privacy or requests concerning your information, contact the service operator using the contact details provided on the website.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Beast AI website operator through the contact method published on the website.
